bustfit

Know if it'll fit, before you buy it

The average bra size is a 34DD. Fashion still doesn't act like it.

Clothes are designed, photographed, and reviewed on smaller busts, leaving millions of women to guess whether something will actually fit. We scroll, we second-guess, we order things that probably won't work. Then we spend more time and money returning them.

bustfit is a free browser extension that analyzes clothing fit for a fuller bust, right while you shop. Open any product page on a supported retailer, and instantly see whether it's likely to work for you, before you spend the money.

It looks at the details that actually matter: construction, fabric, and real reviews from people who've actually worn it, not just a photo that was never going to tell you the truth anyway.

How it works

Get your fit score

A 0-100 score for how likely something is to work for your size, based on real construction and fabric details.

See what actually matters

Bra compatibility, construction details, and sizing guidance, all in one place, without digging through a hundred reviews yourself.

Hear from people like you

Real reviews from other larger-bust shoppers, filtered to show you what actually applies to your body.

bustfit is currently in private beta, with a public launch coming this fall.
